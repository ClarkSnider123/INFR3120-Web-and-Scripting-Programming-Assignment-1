Banking App

The banking app was an app that allowed you to create bank accounts with unique pins and passwords that you could put money into transfer money between and withdraw. The app could be accessed via the terminal or a graphical user interface.

The auto-clicker was a simple project I made to so I wouldn't have to click my mouse repeatedly when playing games. The code ended up working too well as it would click so often that it would lag most programs I used it on.

Simple Turn Based Combat Game

I made a simple turn-based game with working combat and health. The player starts at level 1 and fights monsters for 10 levels deciding on whether to heal or level up. You would also fight multiple monsters and have to make decisions on which one to attack.

BMI Calculator

I made a simple BMI calculator. You enter your weight and height then based on your BMI it tells you if you are underweight normal weight or overweight.
